Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the single most important asset for local search visibility. It determines whether your business appears in the Google Maps Local 3-Pack — the three businesses prominently displayed above all organic results for local queries. GBP signals account for approximately 36% of all local ranking factors according to Whitespark's annual study.

In 2026, Google has added several new features to GBP — AI-generated business descriptions, enhanced attributes, video posts, and tighter integration with AI Overview. This makes optimization more important and more complex than ever. This guide covers every element you need to optimize.

The Complete GBP Optimization Checklist

1. Business Information (Foundation)

  • Business name: Exact legal name only — keyword stuffing violates guidelines and risks suspension
  • Address: Must match your website, citations, and all other online listings exactly
  • Phone number: Use a local phone number (not toll-free) as your primary — local numbers rank better
  • Website URL: Link to your homepage or a dedicated landing page with matching NAP
  • Hours: Set accurate regular hours AND update for holidays using "Special Hours"
  • Business description: 750 characters maximum. Include your primary keywords naturally, mention your city/area, highlight your unique value proposition
  • Opening date: Set this — Google shows business tenure, and established businesses get a trust boost

2. Categories (Critical for Ranking)

Category selection directly impacts which searches your business appears for. This is the #1 most impactful GBP setting:

  • Primary category: Choose the most specific category that describes your core business. "Personal Injury Attorney" is better than "Lawyer"
  • Secondary categories: Add up to 9 additional relevant categories. Research competitor categories using GMB Spy or Pleper
  • Don't add irrelevant categories — this dilutes your ranking signals and can confuse Google
  • Review categories quarterly — Google adds new categories regularly, and a better-matching one may become available

3. Photos & Videos

Businesses with 100+ photos get 520% more calls and 2,717% more direction requests than average:

  • Upload at least 50 high-quality photos to start — then add 5-10 new photos monthly
  • Include: exterior (3+ angles), interior (5+ areas), team photos, products/services in action
  • Geo-tag photos with your business location metadata before uploading
  • Name photo files descriptively: "dental-office-interior-chicago-il.jpg" (not "IMG_4392.jpg")
  • Upload short videos (30-120 seconds) — office tours, team introductions, service demonstrations
  • Set your cover photo to your best, most representative image
  • Set your logo to a clean, recognizable version of your brand mark

4. GBP Posts: Weekly Content Strategy

GBP posts expire after 7 days, so consistency is key. Post at least weekly with these types:

  • What's New posts: Company updates, new services, team additions
  • Offer posts: Seasonal promotions, limited-time deals (include expiration date)
  • Event posts: Workshops, open houses, community events
  • Product posts: Highlight specific products or services with photos and pricing

💡 Post Optimization Tips

Include a high-quality image (1200x900px), a clear call-to-action button (Call, Book, Learn More), and naturally incorporate 1-2 keywords in the post text. Posts with images get 10x more engagement than text-only posts.

5. Review Management

  • Respond to every review within 24 hours — Google tracks your response rate and speed
  • Include keywords naturally in review responses — "Thank you for choosing us for your dental implant procedure in Chicago!"
  • Generate reviews consistently — aim for 5-15+ new reviews monthly
  • Never offer incentives for reviews — violations risk listing suspension
  • Read our complete guide: Ethical Review Generation Guide

6. Questions & Answers

The Q&A section is a hidden ranking factor most businesses ignore:

  • Pre-seed your Q&A by asking and answering common questions yourself (this is allowed and encouraged)
  • Answer questions with detailed, keyword-rich responses
  • Monitor for new questions and answer them within 24 hours
  • Upvote your best answers to keep them pinned at the top
  • Aim for 15-25 pre-seeded Q&As covering your most common customer questions

7. Attributes, Services & Products

  • Attributes: Enable all relevant attributes (wheelchair accessible, free Wi-Fi, outdoor seating, women-led, veteran-led, etc.)
  • Services: Add every service with detailed descriptions (up to 300 characters each) and pricing
  • Products: For product-based businesses, add your full product catalog with images and prices
  • Booking integration: Connect your appointment scheduling system (if applicable)
  • Messaging: Enable GBP messaging for direct customer communication

Pros & Cons of GBP Optimization

✅ Pros

  • 100% free — GBP costs nothing to set up or maintain
  • Directly impacts Google Maps Local 3-Pack rankings (36% of factors)
  • Drives phone calls, direction requests, and website visits
  • Builds trust through reviews, photos, and activity
  • Feeds data to AI assistants for recommendations
  • Mobile-first — captures "near me" searches on the go
  • Provides valuable customer insights and analytics

❌ Cons

  • Requires consistent weekly effort (posts, responses, updates)
  • Competitors can suggest edits to your listing
  • Google can override your data with user-submitted info
  • Verification process can be slow (5-14 days for postcards)
  • Limited control over how Google displays your info
  • Fake reviews from competitors are a persistent problem
  • Algorithm changes can impact rankings suddenly

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I claim my Google Business Profile?
Go to google.com/business, click "Manage now," search for your business, and follow the verification steps. Google verifies ownership via postcard (5-7 days), phone call, email, or instant verification if you've already verified your website in Google Search Console.
How often should I post on Google Business Profile?
Post at least once per week. GBP posts expire after 7 days, so weekly posting keeps your profile active and signals to Google that your business is engaged. Businesses that post weekly see 520% more calls compared to those that don't post.
Can I have multiple GBP categories?
Yes — 1 primary category and up to 9 secondary categories. Your primary category has the most impact on which searches you appear for, so choose carefully. Only add secondary categories that are genuinely relevant to your business.
How many photos should I upload?
Start with at least 50 high-quality photos, then add 5-10 new photos monthly. Businesses with 100+ photos get 520% more calls and 2,717% more direction requests. Include interior, exterior, team, products, and action shots.
Does Google Business Profile affect SEO?
Absolutely. Your GBP is the primary factor determining your Google Maps Local 3-Pack ranking. GBP signals account for approximately 36% of local ranking factors. An optimized GBP directly improves your visibility in both Maps and local organic results.
What are GBP attributes and should I use them?
Attributes are specific features or qualities of your business (wheelchair accessible, free Wi-Fi, outdoor seating, LGBTQ+ friendly, etc.). Enable all relevant attributes — they appear directly on your listing and help you appear in filtered searches like "restaurants with outdoor seating near me."
