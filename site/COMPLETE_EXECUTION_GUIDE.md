# PART 1: CLIENT ONBOARDING
## Universal Process — Har Service Se Pehle Yeh Karo

### Step 1: Discovery Call (30-60 Minutes)
**Kya karna hai:** Client se call karo aur unka business samjho.

**Questions to Ask:**
```
1. Aapka business kya hai aur kab se hai?
2. Current monthly revenue kya hai?
3. Aapke main competitors kaun hain?
4. Ab tak koi marketing ki hai? Results kya aaye?
5. Aapka ideal customer kaun hai?
6. Monthly marketing budget kya hai?
7. Top 3 goals kya hain? (more calls, more reviews, more traffic?)
8. Koi specific areas/cities target karne hain?
9. Current website hai? Google Business Profile hai?
10. Kitne time mein results expect kar rahe hain?
```

**Example Script:**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time today. Before we start, I'd love to understand your business a bit better. Can you tell me about your ideal customer — who's your best type of client and where do they typically find you?"

### Step 2: Business Audit (Before Signing)
**Tools Needed:**
- **Google Search Console** (agar access mil jaye)
- **Google Business Profile** (search kar ke manually check karo)
- **Semrush / Ahrefs / Ubersuggest** (keyword rankings check)
- **BrightLocal** (citation check)
- **Google PageSpeed Insights** (website speed)
- **Screaming Frog** (technical SEO audit)

**Audit Checklist:**
```
□ Google "business name + city" — kya show ho raha hai?
□ Google Maps mein search karo — rank kya hai?
□ GBP profile check — photos, categories, posts, reviews?
□ Website load speed check
□ Mobile responsive check
□ Top 10 keywords ke rankings check
□ Competitor comparison (top 3 competitors)
□ Current review count & rating
□ Social media presence check
□ Current backlinks check
```

### Step 3: Proposal & Agreement
**Proposal Template Structure:**
```
1. Executive Summary — Client ka problem + our solution
2. Audit Findings — What we found
3. Recommended Strategy — What we'll do
4. Deliverables — Exact list of what they'll get
5. Timeline — Month-by-month milestones
6. Pricing — Package options
7. Terms — Month-to-month, 30-day notice
8. Next Steps — Sign & start
```

### Step 4: Onboarding Form
**Client se yeh cheezein collect karo:**
```
□ Business name, address, phone (NAP)
□ All login credentials (Google, Facebook, Yelp, etc.)
□ Google Analytics access (add as user)
□ Google Search Console access
□ Google Business Profile access (add as manager)
□ Website CMS access (WordPress/Wix/etc.)
□ Facebook/Instagram Business Manager access
□ Google Ads account access (if running ads)
□ List of services/products offered
□ Business hours
□ Service areas/cities
□ 20-30 high-quality photos
□ Logo files (PNG, SVG)
□ Target keywords list (if they have one)
□ Previous marketing reports
```

### Step 5: Kickoff Call
- Audit results present karo
- 90-day roadmap share karo
- Set expectations: "SEO takes 3-6 months for significant results"
- Communication schedule set karo (weekly/bi-weekly updates)

# SERVICE 1: LOCAL SEO
## Complete Execution Guide — Start to Finish

### MONTH 1: Foundation (Setup & Audit)

#### Week 1: Deep Technical Audit

**Step 1: Keyword Research**
```
Tools: Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest

Process:
1. Open Semrush → Keyword Magic Tool
2. Type client's main service + city
   Example: "dentist new york"
3. Export all related keywords
4. Filter by:
   - Search volume > 50/month
   - Keyword difficulty < 60
   - Local intent (contains "near me", city name, "best", etc.)
5. Categorize keywords:

   PRIMARY (Homepage):
   - dentist new york (2,400/mo)
   - best dentist nyc (1,200/mo)
   - dental clinic manhattan (800/mo)

   SERVICE PAGES:
   - teeth whitening new york (500/mo)
   - dental implants nyc (400/mo)
   - emergency dentist manhattan (300/mo)

   BLOG CONTENT:
   - how much do dental implants cost nyc (200/mo)
   - best teeth whitening options 2026 (150/mo)

6. Create Master Keyword Spreadsheet:
   | Keyword | Volume | Difficulty | Current Rank | Target Page | Priority |
   |---------|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
   | dentist new york | 2,400 | 45 | Not ranked | Homepage | High |
   | teeth whitening nyc | 500 | 32 | #18 | Service page | Medium |
```

**Step 2: Technical SEO Audit**
```
Tools: Screaming Frog, Google PageSpeed Insights, GTmetrix

Process:
1. Screaming Frog → Enter website URL → Start Crawl
2. Check for:
   □ Broken links (404 errors) → Fix or redirect
   □ Missing title tags → Write unique titles
   □ Missing meta descriptions → Write compelling descriptions
   □ Missing H1 tags → Add one H1 per page
   □ Duplicate content → Canonicalize or rewrite
   □ Missing alt text on images → Add descriptive alt text
   □ Page load speed > 3 seconds → Optimize
   □ Mobile responsiveness issues → Fix
   □ Missing Schema markup → Add LocalBusiness schema
   □ Missing sitemap.xml → Create and submit
   □ Missing robots.txt → Create

3. Google PageSpeed Insights → Run for mobile + desktop
   Target: 90+ score on both

4. Create Technical Audit Report with priorities:
   CRITICAL (Fix immediately):
   - 3 broken links found
   - Homepage missing H1 tag
   - No Schema markup

   HIGH (Fix within 1 week):
   - 5 images missing alt text
   - Page speed: 65/100 (needs optimization)

   MEDIUM (Fix within 2 weeks):
   - Meta descriptions missing on 4 pages
   - No sitemap.xml found
```

**Step 3: Competitor Analysis**
```
Tools: Semrush, Ahrefs, SpyFu

Process:
1. Identify top 5 competitors:
   - Google "dentist [city]" → note top 5 organic results
   - Google Maps "dentist [city]" → note top 3 map results

2. For each competitor, analyze:
   □ Domain Authority (Ahrefs DA)
   □ Number of backlinks
   □ Top ranking keywords
   □ Content strategy (how many blog posts?)
   □ Google reviews count & rating
   □ Citation count
   □ Social media followers

3. Create Competitor Comparison Matrix:
   | Factor | Client | Competitor 1 | Competitor 2 | Competitor 3 |
   |--------|--------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
   | DA | 15 | 35 | 28 | 42 |
   | Backlinks | 50 | 350 | 200 | 500 |
   | Reviews | 23 | 150 | 89 | 210 |
   | Blog Posts | 5 | 45 | 30 | 60 |
   | Map Rank | Not ranked | #1 | #3 | #2 |

4. Identify gaps = our opportunities
```

```

# SERVICE 3: REPUTATION MANAGEMENT
## Complete Execution Guide

### Setup Phase (Week 1-2)

**Step 1: Review Audit**
```
Check ALL platforms:
□ Google Reviews (most important)
□ Yelp
□ Facebook Reviews
□ Trustpilot
□ BBB
□ Industry-specific:
  - Healthgrades, Zocdoc (healthcare)
  - Avvo (lawyers)
  - TripAdvisor (restaurants)
  - HomeAdvisor, Angi (home services)

Create Review Audit Spreadsheet:
| Platform | Total Reviews | Rating | Last Review Date | Negative Reviews |
|----------|--------------|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Google   | 23           | 4.2    | 2 months ago    | 3               |
| Yelp     | 8            | 3.5    | 6 months ago    | 2               |
| Facebook | 12           | 4.5    | 1 month ago     | 1               |

Key Findings:
- Total reviews across all platforms: 43
- Average rating: 4.1
- Biggest gap: Low review volume on Google
- Negative review themes: Wait times, billing confusion
```

**Step 2: Review Generation System Setup**
```
Tools: Podium, Birdeye, NiceJob, or Grade.us (paid)
Budget-friendly: Google review link + email/SMS automation

GOOGLE REVIEW LINK CREATION:
1. Go to Google Maps → Find client's business
2. Click "Write a review" → Copy the URL
3. Or use: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[PLACE_ID]
4. Shorten with bit.ly or custom domain

REVIEW REQUEST METHODS:

Method 1: Post-Service SMS (Highest Conversion ~20-30%)
  Timing: Send 1-2 hours after service
  Message:
  "Hi [Name]! Thank you for choosing SmileCare NYC today. We'd love to hear
  about your experience! Could you take 30 seconds to leave us a review?
  [SHORT LINK] — Thank you! 😊"

Method 2: Email Follow-Up (Conversion ~5-10%)
  Timing: Send same evening or next morning
  Subject: "How was your visit to SmileCare NYC?"
  Body:
  "Dear [Name],

  Thank you for visiting SmileCare NYC today. We hope you had a great
  experience with Dr. [Name].

  If you have a moment, we'd be incredibly grateful if you could share
  your experience on Google. It only takes 30 seconds and helps other
  families find quality dental care.

  [LEAVE A REVIEW BUTTON]

  Thank you for being a valued patient!
  The SmileCare NYC Team"

Method 3: In-Office QR Code
  - Print QR code linking to Google review page
  - Place at reception desk, in treatment rooms, on receipts
  - Train staff to say: "If you had a great experience today,
    we'd love it if you could scan this QR code and leave us a review."

Method 4: Review Request Cards
  - Business card-sized cards
  - Hand to clients after positive interactions
  - Front: "Thank you for choosing us!"
  - Back: QR code + short URL + simple instructions

AUTOMATION FLOW (Using Podium/Birdeye):
1. Client visits → entered into CRM
2. 2 hours later → automated SMS sent
3. If no action → 24 hours later → email sent
4. If no action → 1 week later → final reminder
5. If negative response → routes to private feedback form (NOT public review)

IMPORTANT — NEVER:
❌ Buy reviews
❌ Offer incentives for reviews (discount, gift card)
❌ Write reviews yourself or for clients
❌ Use review farms or fiverr services
❌ Ask only happy customers (review gating is against Google policy)
❌ Copy/paste same review text for multiple reviewers
```

**Step 3: Negative Review Management**
```
PROCESS FOR HANDLING NEGATIVE REVIEWS:

1. DON'T PANIC — Respond within 24 hours
2. Acknowledge their experience
3. Apologize (even if you disagree)
4. Take the conversation offline
5. Try to resolve the issue
6. If resolved, politely ask if they'd update their review

Response Framework (A.R.E.S.):
- Acknowledge: "We're sorry to hear about your experience"
