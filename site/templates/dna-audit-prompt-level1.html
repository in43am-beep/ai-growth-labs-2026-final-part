THE MEGA PROMPT — Copy Below

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  AI SEO AGENCY — COMPLETE DNA-LEVEL WEBSITE AUDIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

You are a senior SEO strategist and technical SEO auditor with 15+ years of experience working at top agencies (like WebFX, Victorious, NP Digital). You have deep expertise in all 12 DNA pillars of website ranking.

I need you to perform a COMPLETE, PROFESSIONAL-GRADE DNA-LEVEL SEO AUDIT of this website:

🔗 WEBSITE URL: [WEBSITE_URL]

--- INSTRUCTIONS ---

Browse the website thoroughly. Visit at minimum: the homepage, 3-5 inner pages (services, about, contact, blog if exists), and any landing pages. Analyze everything you can see publicly. Be extremely detailed and specific — give exact findings, not generic advice.

Produce a STRUCTURED AUDIT REPORT covering ALL 12 DNA PILLARS below. For each factor, give:
- Current status (what you found)
- Score out of 10
- Specific issues found
- Priority fix recommendation

Use this EXACT report structure:

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 1: TECHNICAL SEO DNA (Foundation)
══════════════════════════════════════

A) Crawlability Analysis
- Check: robots.txt rules, crawl blocks, orphan pages, crawl depth, broken links, redirect chains
- Report any pages that might be blocked from Google

B) Indexability Analysis
- Check: Are important pages likely indexed? Look for noindex signals, canonical issues, duplicate content patterns, thin pages, soft 404 patterns
- Estimate how many pages should vs shouldn't be indexed

C) Site Speed & Core Web Vitals
- Analyze: page load perception, heavy resources visible (large images, unoptimized videos, render-blocking scripts)
- Check for: excessive JavaScript, uncompressed images, no lazy loading, layout shifts visible
- Estimate LCP, CLS, INP risk based on page structure
- Score each Core Web Vital: LCP / CLS / INP / TTFB

D) Mobile Optimization
- Check: responsive design quality, tap target sizes, mobile navigation, mobile layout issues, font readability on mobile
- Is this truly mobile-first or desktop-adapted?

E) Site Architecture
- Analyze: URL structure pattern, hierarchy depth, breadcrumb implementation, internal navigation logic, click depth to important pages
- Is the architecture flat or deep? Is it logical?

F) Security
- Check: HTTPS implementation, SSL certificate, mixed content warnings, any security red flags

G) Structured Data / Schema Markup
- Check for: LocalBusiness, Organization, FAQPage, Review/AggregateRating, Service, Article, BreadcrumbList, HowTo, VideoObject, Person schema
- What's implemented? What's missing? What's broken?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 2: ON-PAGE SEO DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

A) Title Tags
- Check homepage + 3-5 inner page titles
- Analyze: keyword placement, CTR optimization, uniqueness, length (under 60 chars?), brand inclusion
- Are titles compelling or generic?

B) Meta Descriptions
- Check: are they present? Unique per page? Include CTR hooks? Proper length (under 160 chars)?
- Are they engaging or auto-generated?

C) Heading Structure (H1/H2/H3)
- Analyze heading hierarchy on homepage + key pages
- Check: proper H1 usage (single H1?), semantic structure, keyword coverage in headings, topic depth through subheadings

D) Keyword Optimization
- Identify: what primary keywords the site appears to target
- Check: keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, URL, throughout content
- Assess: keyword stuffing vs natural usage
- Check for: secondary/semantic keyword variety, NLP entity coverage

E) Content Quality Assessment
- Analyze: originality (does it feel AI-generated or human-written?), helpfulness, depth, expertise signals (E-E-A-T)
- Word count estimates for key pages
- Is content providing real value or thin/generic?

F) Image & Media Optimization
- Check: image alt text present?, file naming conventions, image compression (oversized images?), video embeds optimized?, WebP/AVIF usage
- Count approximate images per page and optimization status

G) Internal Linking
- Check: are pages well-connected? Topical linking present? Orphan pages visible? Anchor text quality?
- Is there a strategic internal linking structure or random links?

H) UX Signals
- Analyze: readability (font size, contrast, spacing), CTA placement and design, engagement elements, bounce risk factors, above-the-fold content quality

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 3: CONTENT SEO DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

A) Topic Coverage
- Does the website comprehensively cover its niche?
- What topics are covered vs missing?
- Is there topical authority building or scattered content?

B) Semantic SEO
- Are related entities properly used throughout content?
- Does the site use semantic language that Google can understand?
- NLP optimization assessment

C) Search Intent Match
- For key pages: does the content match search intent?
- Informational vs commercial vs transactional intent alignment
- Are service pages selling or informing?

D) Content Freshness
- When was content last updated?
- Are there dated references or outdated information?
- Blog posting frequency (if blog exists)

E) Helpful Content Assessment
- Does content actually solve user problems?
- Google Helpful Content System compliance check
- People-first vs search-engine-first content?

F) E-E-A-T Analysis
- Experience: Does content show first-hand experience?
- Expertise: Are credentials, qualifications shown?
- Authoritativeness: Is the site/author recognized in the niche?
- Trust: Contact info, about page, privacy policy, reviews?

G) Content Depth
- Surface-level or authority-level content?
- Compared to top-ranking competitors, is content thinner or more comprehensive?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 4: ENTITY SEO DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

- What entities does this website represent?
- Are entities properly defined and connected?
- Is there a semantic entity graph (related topics logically connected)?
- Entity gap: what entities are competitors covering that this site is not?
- NLP entity optimization level
- Topical map assessment: how well does the site map its topic universe?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 5: INTERNAL LINKING DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

- Are there orphan pages (pages with no internal links pointing to them)?
- Is there a silo/hub structure?
- Topical clusters properly linked?
- Contextual links within content (not just navigation)?
- Anchor text diversity and optimization
- Click depth: can users reach important pages within 3 clicks?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 6: OFF-PAGE SEO DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

A) Backlink Profile Estimate
- Based on domain authority signals, content quality, and niche — estimate the likely backlink strength
- Are there obvious link-worthy assets on the site?

B) Brand Presence
- Google the brand name: what comes up?
- Is there a Knowledge Panel?
- Brand mentions visible across the web?

C) Citation Profile (for local businesses)
- Check for NAP (Name, Address, Phone) on the website
- Is NAP prominent and consistent?
- Estimated citation presence

D) Social Signals
- Check for social media links on the website
- Visit each linked social profile and analyze:
  * Platform name and URL
  * Follower/subscriber count
  * Last post date
  * Posting frequency
  * Engagement level (likes, comments, shares per post)
  * Content quality and consistency
  * Is the account active or dormant?
  * Profile completeness (bio, profile pic, cover photo, contact info)

Check these platforms (if linked or findable):
  - Facebook (page likes, reviews, post engagement)
  - Instagram (followers, post frequency, reel views, engagement rate)
  - YouTube (subscribers, video count, view counts, last upload)
  - TikTok (followers, video views, posting frequency)
  - Twitter/X (followers, tweet frequency, engagement)
  - LinkedIn (company page followers, post activity)
  - Pinterest (monthly views, pin count, board organization)

For each platform found, report:
  ☐ Profile URL
  ☐ Follower count
  ☐ Last post date
  ☐ Posts per week/month
  ☐ Average engagement
  ☐ Active or Dormant?
  ☐ Content quality (1-10)
  ☐ Profile completeness (1-10)
  ☐ Why it's failing / succeeding

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 7: LOCAL SEO DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

A) Google Business Profile
- Search "[business name] + [city]" on Google
- Check: Does GBP listing appear? Rating? Review count? Photo count?
- Analyze: categories, business description quality, post activity, Q&A
- Check for negative reviews — list the top 3 most critical negative reviews and what they say
- Are review responses present? Quality of responses?

B) NAP Consistency
- Is Name, Address, Phone consistent on website (header, footer, contact page)?
- Local phone number vs toll-free?
- Address format consistent?

C) Local Content
- Are there location-specific pages?
- City names in titles, content, meta?
- Service area properly defined?

D) Geo-Relevance Signals
- Embedded Google Map?
- Local schema markup?
- Local landmarks or area references in content?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 8: USER BEHAVIOR DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

Based on your analysis of the site, estimate risk for:
- High bounce rate (what would make users leave?)
- Low dwell time (is content engaging enough?)
- Pogo-sticking risk (would users go back to Google?)
- Scroll depth concerns (is above-fold content compelling?)
- Engagement quality (interactive elements, clear next steps?)

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 9: CONVERSION DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

- CTA Analysis: Are calls-to-action clear, visible, compelling?
- Trust Signals: reviews displayed, badges, certifications, guarantees?
- Contact Options: phone, form, chat, email — all present?
- Form Quality: how many fields? Friction level?
- Conversion Flow: can a visitor easily go from landing → action?
- Social Proof: testimonials, case studies, review widgets on site?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 10: COMPETITOR DNA ANALYSIS
══════════════════════════════════════

Identify 3-5 likely competitors ranking for the same keywords.
For each competitor, briefly compare:

A) Authority Level (estimated domain strength)
B) Content Gap (what they have that this site doesn't)
C) Keyword Gap (topics/keywords competitors rank for but this site doesn't)
D) Entity Gap (semantic coverage differences)
E) UX Gap (is competitor's user experience stronger?)
F) Topical Depth Gap (who has deeper content?)

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 11: INDEXING DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

- Do a "site:[domain]" search on Google and report estimated indexed page count
- Are important pages showing up?
- Any obvious thin content or duplicate pages in the index?
- Is there a visible XML sitemap link?
- Any signs of crawl budget waste (parameter URLs, tag pages, etc.)?
- JavaScript rendering concerns (does the site rely heavily on JS for content)?

══════════════════════════════════════
PILLAR 12: AI / PROGRAMMATIC SEO DNA
══════════════════════════════════════

- Is the site using programmatic/template-based pages? (e.g., city+service pages)
- Could the site benefit from programmatic SEO? How?
- AI visibility assessment: search "[business type] in [city]" on ChatGPT/Perplexity — is this business mentioned?
- Is content AI-friendly (structured, factual, directly answering questions)?
- Dynamic content opportunities (FAQ pages, comparison pages, data-driven content)
