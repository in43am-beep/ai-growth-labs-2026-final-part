Discovery Call Script, Requirements & Scoring Guide

Discovery Call Guide

Duration: 30-45 minutes | Use this template for every prospective client call.

PRE-CALL PREPARATION (15 minutes before call)

Research Checklist — Complete Before the Call

  • Google their business name → Check current ranking
  • Check Google Maps ranking for main service + city
  • View their Google Business Profile → Note review count & rating
  • Visit their website → Note speed, mobile, design quality
  • Check competitors (top 3 in maps for their keyword)
  • Note 3 specific improvement opportunities you can mention
  • Have pricing sheet ready
  • Have Calendly link ready for follow-up booking

CALL SCRIPT — Phase by Phase

0:00 - 3:00 — Phase 1: Warm Welcome & Rapport

YOU: "Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time to chat today! Before we dive in, I just want to set expectations — this call is all about understanding your business and seeing if we can help. No pressure, no hard sell. Sound good?"

[Wait for response]

YOU: "Great! So tell me a little bit about yourself — how did you get started with [business type]?"

[Let them talk. Build rapport. Find common ground. 2-3 minutes of casual conversation.]
💡 Pro Tip: People buy from people they like. Spend genuine time here. Ask about their story. Don't rush into your pitch.

3:00 - 12:00 — Phase 2: Understanding Their Business

YOU: "Now let me ask you some questions about your business so I can understand your situation better..."

Q1: "What services does your business offer, and which ones are the most profitable for you?"
[Notes: _______________]

Q2: "What area do you serve? Is it a specific city, or multiple locations?"
[Notes: _______________]

Q3: "How are you currently getting new customers or leads? What's working and what's not?"
[Notes: _______________]

Q4: "How many new customers/leads are you getting per month right now?"
[Notes: _______________]

Q5: "What's the average value of a new customer for your business?"
[Notes: Average customer value = $___. This helps calculate ROI later.]

Q6: "Have you worked with any marketing agency or SEO company before? What was that experience like?"
[Notes: Listen for pain points — this tells you what NOT to do]

Q7: "Who are your main competitors? Who do you see showing up above you on Google?"
[Notes: _______________]

12:00 - 18:00 — Phase 3: Identifying Pain Points & Goals

YOU: "That's really helpful. Now, what are the biggest challenges you're facing right now when it comes to growing your business online?"

[Let them talk. Note their main frustrations.]

Q8: "If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing about your online presence, what would it be?"
[Notes: This reveals their #1 priority]

Q9: "Where do you want your business to be 12 months from now? More customers? More locations? More revenue?"
[Notes: _______________]

Q10: "What would it mean to your business if you were getting 50% more leads from Google every month?"
[Notes: This gets them to visualize success and anchor the value]
💡 Pro Tip: The more they talk about their pain, the more they'll value your solution. Don't rush to present — let them FEEL the problem first.

18:00 - 28:00 — Phase 4: Present Your Solution

YOU: "Thank you for sharing all of that. I actually did some research on your business before this call, and I noticed a few things..."

[Share 3 specific findings:]
"1. When I searched [service + city], your business wasn't showing up in the Google Maps top 3. Your competitors [Name] and [Name] are currently there."

"2. You currently have [X] Google reviews with a [X] star rating. Your top competitor has [X] reviews at [X] stars. Reviews are one of the biggest ranking factors."

"3. Your website [specific issue — slow, not mobile-friendly, missing Schema, thin content, etc.]"

YOU: "Here's the good news — these are all fixable, and this is exactly what we do every day. Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend..."

[Present the recommended package — customize based on their needs and budget]

YOU: "For your situation, our [Package Name] would be the best fit. This includes: [list 4-5 key deliverables]. The investment is [price]/month with no long-term contracts — we're month-to-month because we believe in earning your business through results."

28:00 - 35:00 — Phase 5: Handle Objections & Close

Common Objections & Responses

"It's too expensive."

YOU: "I totally understand. Let me put it in perspective — you said a new customer is worth about $[X] to your business. If we get you just [Y] extra customers per month, that's $[Z] in revenue. Your investment of $[price] would pay for itself [X] times over. Would that kind of ROI make sense for your business?"

"I need to think about it."

YOU: "Absolutely, take your time. Can I ask — what specifically do you need to think about? Is it the budget, the services, or something else? I want to make sure I've given you everything you need to make a decision."

"I tried SEO before and it didn't work."

YOU: "That's actually really common, and I'm sorry you had that experience. Can you tell me what they did? [Listen]. The difference with us is [specific differentiator — AI-powered, transparent reporting, month-to-month, etc.]. We also do a free audit first so you can see exactly what we'll do before you commit a dollar."

"Can you guarantee results?"

YOU: "I want to be completely honest with you — no ethical agency can guarantee a #1 ranking because Google's algorithm has hundreds of factors. What I CAN guarantee is that we'll do everything in our power to improve your visibility, and we'll show you measurable progress every month. If you don't see value, you can cancel anytime — that's why we're month-to-month."

35:00 - 40:00 — Phase 6: Next Steps

If they're ready:
"Great! Here's what happens next: I'll send you our proposal and onboarding form today. Once you fill that out and we have access to your accounts, we can start within [X] days. I'll also send you a Calendly link to book our kickoff call."

If they need time:
"No problem at all. I'll send you a detailed proposal with everything we discussed today. Can I follow up with you on [specific day]? And in the meantime, I'll send you a free mini-audit of your Google Business Profile so you can see the kind of insights we provide."

Always end with:
"[Name], I really appreciate your time today. Regardless of whether we work together, I hope the insights from this call were valuable. Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions."

LEAD QUALIFICATION SCORING

Score each prospect to prioritize follow-ups:

FactorScoreCriteria
Budget/2525 = $3K+/mo | 15 = $1-3K | 5 = Under $1K
Urgency/2525 = Needs results now | 15 = Soon | 5 = Exploring
Decision Maker/2525 = Is the owner | 15 = Manager | 5 = Employee
Business Fit/2525 = Perfect niche | 15 = Good fit | 5 = Outside expertise
Total Score: ___/100

80-100: HOT LEAD — Send proposal within 2 hours. Follow up within 24 hours.

50-79: WARM LEAD — Send proposal within 24 hours. Follow up within 3 days.

Below 50: COLD LEAD — Add to nurture sequence. Follow up monthly.

POST-CALL ACTIONS

  • Update CRM with call notes and lead score
  • Send personalized follow-up email within 2 hours
  • Send proposal (if qualified)
  • Send free mini-audit (builds trust)
  • Set follow-up reminder in calendar
  • Add to email nurture sequence

Remember: The discovery call is about THEM, not you. Listen 70%, talk 30%.
