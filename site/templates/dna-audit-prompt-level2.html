PROMPT A: GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE DEEP AUDIT

Use when you have GSC data exported or when using AI tools connected to GSC.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE — DNA DEEP AUDIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

You are a senior SEO strategist. I'm giving you Google Search Console data for my client's website. Perform a PROFESSIONAL-GRADE analysis.

🔗 WEBSITE: [WEBSITE_URL]
📊 GSC DATA PERIOD: Last 3 months (or 6/12/16 months if available)

I will paste or upload the following GSC data:
1. Performance Report (Queries tab) — top keywords with impressions, clicks, CTR, position
2. Performance Report (Pages tab) — top pages with traffic data
3. Coverage/Indexing Report — indexed, excluded, errors
4. Sitemaps status
5. Core Web Vitals report
6. Mobile Usability report

═══ ANALYZE THE FOLLOWING: ═══

1. KEYWORD INTELLIGENCE
From the Queries data:
- Identify TOP 20 money keywords (commercial intent, driving leads)
- Identify TOP 20 opportunity keywords (position 5-20, high impressions, low CTR)
- Identify DECLINING keywords (position dropping month-over-month)
- Identify QUICK WINS (position 4-10, just need small push to page 1)
- Calculate: which keywords have abnormally low CTR for their position? (title/meta issue)
- Group keywords by: service type, location, intent

For each opportunity keyword, provide:
| Keyword | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Position | Opportunity Type | Fix |
|---------|------------|--------|-----|----------|-----------------|-----|

2. PAGE PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
From the Pages data:
- TOP 10 performing pages (traffic drivers)
- UNDERPERFORMING pages (high impressions, low clicks = bad titles/metas)
- DEAD pages (zero or near-zero traffic — fix or remove?)
- Pages losing traffic month-over-month (content decay)
- Pages with high CTR (what makes them work? replicate pattern)
- CANNIBALIZATION check: are multiple pages competing for same keywords?

3. INDEXING HEALTH
From Coverage/Indexing data:
- Total indexed pages vs total pages on site
- Excluded pages — WHY? (noindex, crawl anomaly, redirect, duplicate, soft 404)
- Error pages — list every error with fix recommendation
- Are important pages missing from the index?
- Index bloat: are unimportant pages being indexed? (tags, categories, parameters)
- Sitemap coverage: submitted vs indexed

4. TECHNICAL HEALTH FROM GSC
- Core Web Vitals pass/fail rates (mobile + desktop)
- Mobile usability issues list with fix recommendations
- Crawl stats analysis (if available): pages crawled/day, response time, size
- Security issues flagged
- Manual actions (if any)

5. CTR OPTIMIZATION PLAN
For every keyword in position 1-10 with below-average CTR:
| Keyword | Position | Current CTR | Expected CTR | Gap | Current Title | Recommended Title |
|---------|----------|-------------|-------------|-----|--------------|-------------------|

6. CONTENT STRATEGY FROM DATA
Based on keyword data:
- What topics should the client create content for NEXT?
- What existing content should be UPDATED/EXPANDED?
- What content should be MERGED (cannibalization fixes)?
- What content should be REMOVED (thin, no traffic, hurting quality)?

7. 90-DAY PRIORITY ROADMAP
Based on ALL GSC findings, create:
Month 1: [Quick wins + critical fixes]
Month 2: [Content optimization + gap filling]
Month 3: [Authority building + expansion]

Each item should have: task, keyword/page affected, estimated traffic impact.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  FORMAT: Use tables wherever possible. Be specific with page URLs
  and keyword names. This report goes directly to the client.

PROMPT B: GOOGLE ANALYTICS (GA4) DEEP AUDIT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  GOOGLE ANALYTICS (GA4) — TRAFFIC & BEHAVIOR AUDIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

You are a senior digital marketing analyst. Analyze this GA4 data for my client's website and provide a professional report.

🔗 WEBSITE: [WEBSITE_URL]
📊 GA4 DATA PERIOD: Last 3 months

I will paste or upload GA4 data including:
1. Overview metrics (users, sessions, engagement rate, conversions)
2. Traffic Acquisition (channels: organic, direct, referral, social, paid)
3. User Acquisition (new vs returning)
4. Engagement (pages/session, average engagement time, events)
5. Top Landing Pages (sessions, engagement, conversions per page)
6. Demographics (if available: age, gender, location)
7. Tech (devices: mobile/desktop/tablet split)
8. Conversion events (form submissions, calls, bookings)

═══ ANALYZE THE FOLLOWING: ═══

1. TRAFFIC HEALTH ANALYSIS
- Overall traffic trend (growing, stable, declining?)
- Traffic channel breakdown with percentages
- Which channel is underperforming vs potential?
- Month-over-month comparison for each channel
- Identify: where are the BIGGEST growth opportunities?

2. USER BEHAVIOR INTELLIGENCE
- Engagement rate (above or below 50% benchmark?)
- Average engagement time (above or below 1 minute benchmark?)
- Pages per session (above or below 2 benchmark?)
- Bounce risk pages (low engagement, high exit rate)
- High-value pages (long engagement, high conversion)
- Device split analysis — is mobile experience hurting conversions?

3. CONVERSION ANALYSIS
- Total conversions by type (calls, forms, bookings, etc.)
- Conversion rate by channel (which channel converts best?)
- Conversion rate by device (mobile vs desktop gap?)
- Conversion rate by landing page (which pages convert best?)
- Conversion funnel: where are users dropping off?
- Cost per conversion estimate (if paid data available)

4. LANDING PAGE PERFORMANCE
- Top 10 landing pages by traffic + engagement + conversions
- Worst performing landing pages (fix or redirect?)
- Pages with high traffic but low conversion (UX/CTA issue)
- Pages with low traffic but high conversion (need more visibility)

5. AUDIENCE INSIGHTS
- Geographic hotspots (which cities/regions drive most traffic?)
- Device breakdown and impact on conversion
- New vs returning user behavior differences
- Peak traffic times (when should content/ads be published?)

6. CHANNEL-SPECIFIC RECOMMENDATIONS
For each traffic channel, provide:
- Current performance summary
- Growth opportunity
- Specific action items

7. DATA-DRIVEN STRATEGY
Based on ALL GA4 findings:
- Top 5 most impactful changes to make
- Content/pages to prioritize
- UX improvements needed
- Conversion optimization recommendations
- 90-day growth plan tied to data

  FORMAT: Professional report with tables and charts descriptions.
  All recommendations must be tied to specific data points.
