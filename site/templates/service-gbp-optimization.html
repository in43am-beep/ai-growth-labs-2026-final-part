GBP Optimization — Complete Execution Playbook

Step-by-step instructions for delivering GBP optimization service from Day 1 to ongoing management.

WEEK 1: Profile Claim, Verification & Full Setup

1Claim & Verify the Profile

Process:

  1. Go to business.google.com
  2. Search for the client's business by name + address
  3. If listed but unclaimed → "Claim this business" → Follow prompts
  4. If not listed → "Add your business to Google"
  5. Choose verification method:
    MethodSpeedWhen Available
    Phone callInstantEstablished businesses with verified phone
    EmailInstantBusinesses with domain email
    Video verification1-3 daysMost businesses
    Postcard5-14 daysAlways available (last resort)
💡 Try phone/email verification first. If not available, video verification is faster than postcard.

2Complete 100% Profile Optimization

BASIC INFORMATION — Fill Every Field

  • Business Name — Exact legal name only (NO keyword stuffing!)
    CORRECT: SmileCare NYC WRONG: SmileCare NYC - Best Dentist New York Teeth Whitening Dental Implants
  • Primary Category — Most specific option available (e.g., "Cosmetic Dentist" not just "Dentist")
  • Secondary Categories — Add ALL relevant (up to 10): Dentist, Pediatric Dentist, Emergency Dental Service, etc.
  • Address — Exact match everywhere (same format on website, citations, everywhere)
  • Phone — Local number preferred over toll-free
  • Website URL — Add UTM parameters:
    https://yoursite.com?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp
  • Business Hours — Accurate + update for holidays
  • Special Hours — Add for holidays, events, seasonal changes

BUSINESS DESCRIPTION (750 characters max)

Template: [Business Name] is a [type] serving [location] since [year]. Our [team/experienced staff] provides [top 3-4 services]. Located in [neighborhood/area], we offer [unique selling point]. [Insurance/payment info if applicable]. [CTA]. Example: SmileCare NYC is a family and cosmetic dental practice serving Manhattan and surrounding areas since 2010. Our team of experienced dentists provides comprehensive care including teeth whitening, dental implants, Invisalign, emergency dental services, and preventive dentistry. Located in the heart of Midtown, we offer same-day appointments and accept most major insurance plans. Book your free consultation today.

4Q&A Management

Seed 15+ Q&As in First Month:

Use a separate Google account to ask questions, then answer from the business profile.

Q&A Examples to Seed: Q: Do you accept dental insurance? A: Yes! SmileCare NYC accepts most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, and Guardian. We also offer flexible payment plans for uninsured patients. Call (212) 555-0123. Q: Do you offer emergency dental services? A: Yes, we provide same-day emergency dental appointments in Manhattan. For severe tooth pain, broken teeth, or any dental emergency, call us immediately at (212) 555-0123. Q: What are your hours? A: We're open Monday-Friday 9AM-6PM and Saturday 10AM-2PM. We also offer early morning and evening appointments by request. Q: Do you offer financing? A: Yes! We offer flexible financing through CareCredit and in-house payment plans. Most treatments can be broken into affordable monthly payments with 0% interest. Q: Is parking available? A: Yes, there is a parking garage directly adjacent to our building at 123 Broadway. We also validate parking for patients.
⚠️ Important: Upvote your answers so they stay at the top. Monitor Q&A weekly for new questions from the public — answer within 24 hours.

5Review Management

Review Response Templates:

5-STAR RESPONSE: "Thank you so much, [Name]! We're thrilled you had such a wonderful experience at SmileCare NYC. [Reference their specific comment]. We look forward to seeing you again!" 4-STAR RESPONSE: "Thank you for your kind review, [Name]! We're glad you enjoyed your visit. If there's anything we can do to earn that 5th star next time, please let us know!" 3-STAR RESPONSE: "Thank you for sharing your feedback, [Name]. We appreciate your honesty and would love to hear more about how we can improve. Please reach out to us at (212) 555-0123." 1-2 STAR RESPONSE: "We're sorry to hear about your experience, [Name]. This isn't the standard we hold ourselves to. We'd like to make this right — please contact our office manager directly at (212) 555-0123 or info@smilecarenyc.com."

Response Rules:

  • Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours (ideally 6 hours)
  • Always include the business name in responses (helps SEO)
  • Reference specific details from their review (shows you read it)
  • Never argue, get defensive, or expose patient information (HIPAA)
  • Never offer incentives/discounts in review responses
  • Take negative conversations offline — provide phone/email
