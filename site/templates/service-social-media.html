Social Media Management — Complete Execution Playbook

PHASE 1: ACCOUNT SETUP & OPTIMIZATION (Week 1)

1Platform Profile Optimization

Instagram Business Profile:

Bio Formula (150 chars): Line 1: What you do + emoji Line 2: Location Line 3: Unique selling point Line 4: CTA Example: 🦷 Family & Cosmetic Dentistry 📍 Manhattan, New York ⭐ 4.9 Stars — 200+ Reviews 👇 Book FREE Consultation Profile Photo: Logo (clear at small size) Username: @smilecarenyc (consistent across all platforms) Link in Bio: Linktree or custom landing page with: - Book appointment - Leave a review - View services - Contact us - Special offers Highlights (create 5-7): 1. 🦷 Services — All services overview 2. ⭐ Reviews — Screenshot best Google reviews 3. 📸 Before/After — Transformations 4. 👥 Team — Meet the doctors 5. ❓ FAQ — Common questions answered 6. 🎉 Offers — Current promotions 7. 📍 Visit Us — Office tour, directions

Facebook Business Page:

  • Cover photo (820x312px) — branded, professional
  • Profile photo — logo
  • Complete About section with ALL business info
  • Add all services with descriptions and pricing
  • Enable Reviews/Recommendations
  • Set up Messenger auto-reply
  • Add CTA button: "Book Now" → Link to booking page
  • Create a Facebook Group for community (optional)

TikTok Business:

  • Business account (access to analytics)
  • Bio: Same formula as Instagram
  • Link to website
  • Content focus: Educational, behind-the-scenes, trending audio

LinkedIn Company Page:

  • Complete company info, logo, cover image
  • Detailed About section
  • Add all employees as team members
  • Best for: B2B services, professional services (lawyers, consultants)

2Content Pillars

5 Content Pillars (adjust percentages per industry): 1. EDUCATIONAL (40%) Tips, how-tos, myths vs facts, industry insights Examples: - "5 foods that secretly stain your teeth" - "When to call a plumber vs DIY fix" - "3 things your lawyer wants you to know" 2. BEHIND THE SCENES (20%) Day-in-the-life, team intros, office culture, process Examples: - "A day in Dr. Smith's life" - "How we prepare for your appointment" - "Meet our new team member!" 3. SOCIAL PROOF (20%) Testimonials, before/after, case studies, reviews Examples: - Patient testimonial video - Before/after smile transformation - Screenshot of 5-star Google review 4. PROMOTIONAL (10%) Offers, new services, booking reminders Examples: - "New patient special — this month only!" - "Now offering Saturday appointments" - "Limited spots available this week" 5. ENGAGING/FUN (10%) Polls, quizzes, trending audio, humor, holidays Examples: - "This or that: Invisalign vs Braces?" - "Guess the dental tool!" quiz - Holiday greetings, team celebrations

4Content Creation Process (Batch Method)

BATCH CREATION DAY (1 day per month = 30 days of content) Morning (9AM-12PM): Plan + Write 1. Review last month's top-performing posts 2. Research trending topics, audio, hashtags 3. Write all 30 captions for the month 4. Create content briefs for videos Afternoon (1PM-4PM): Design + Create 5. Open Canva → Create 20 static/carousel posts - Use brand templates - Maintain consistent visual style - Export as PNG (1080x1080 for feed, 1080x1920 for stories/reels) 6. Film 8-10 short videos (batch filming) - Use phone camera (front-facing for personality) - Natural lighting, clean background - 15-60 seconds each Evening (4PM-6PM): Edit + Schedule 7. Edit videos in CapCut: - Add captions/subtitles (85% watch without sound!) - Add trending audio - Add text overlays - Trim and polish 8. Upload everything to scheduling tool (Later, Buffer, or Meta Suite) 9. Set posting times, add hashtags, add locations 10. Done for the month! Tools Needed: - Canva Pro ($13/mo) — Design - CapCut (Free) — Video editing - Later ($25/mo) or Buffer — Scheduling - Phone with good camera — Filming - Ring light ($20) — Better lighting

5Hashtag Strategy

Hashtag Formula (20-25 per post): Large (500K+ posts) — 3-5: #dentist #dentalcare #smile #oralhealth #healthyteeth Medium (50K-500K) — 5-10: #cosmeticdentist #teethwhitening #dentistlife #dentalimplants #smiledesign Small/Niche (5K-50K) — 5-10: #nycdentist #manhattandentist #nycsmiles #newyorkdentist #midtowndentist Branded (create your own) — 3-5: #SmileCareNYC #SmileCareSmile #SmileCareResults Local — 2-3: #manhattan #nyc #newyorkcity Save 5-6 hashtag sets in your notes for quick copy-paste: Set 1: General dental Set 2: Cosmetic/whitening Set 3: Emergency/implants Set 4: Behind the scenes Set 5: Testimonials Set 6: Location-specific

PHASE 3: COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT (Daily)

6Daily Engagement Checklist (20-30 min)

  • Respond to ALL comments on posts within 4 hours
  • Respond to ALL DMs within 2 hours
  • Like comments from followers
  • Engage with 10-15 posts from target audience/local businesses
  • Engage with 5 posts from each hashtag set
  • Check mentions and tags → Repost to Stories
  • Reply to Stories mentions
  • Post 3-5 Stories (mix of content, polls, behind-scenes)
  • Forward any lead inquiries from DMs to client within 1 hour
💡 Engagement is more important than posting. A business that posts 3x/week but engages daily will outperform one that posts daily but never engages.
