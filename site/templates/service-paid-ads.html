SERVICE PLAYBOOK — Facebook & Google Ads Management

Paid Advertising — Complete Execution Playbook

PHASE 1: ACCOUNT SETUP & STRATEGY (Week 1)

1Google Ads Setup

  • Create/access Google Ads account (ads.google.com)
  • Link to Google Analytics 4 (Admin → Google Ads Linking)
  • Link to Google Business Profile
  • Install Google Tag (gtag.js) on website — verify firing
  • Set up conversion tracking:
    Essential Conversions to Track: 1. Phone calls (from ads + from website) → Use call tracking number 2. Form submissions → Track thank-you page visit 3. Chat/messaging → Track chat initiations 4. Direction requests → Track button clicks 5. Appointment bookings → Track confirmation page
  • Set up Enhanced Conversions (first-party data)
  • Create remarketing audiences
  • Set up negative keyword lists

2Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads Setup

  • Create/access Business Manager (business.facebook.com)
  • Verify domain ownership
  • Install Meta Pixel on website — verify events firing
  • Set up Conversions API (CAPI) — server-side tracking
  • Create Custom Audiences:
    Custom Audiences to Build: 1. Website Visitors — Last 30 days 2. Website Visitors — Last 60 days 3. Website Visitors — Last 180 days 4. Page Engagers — Last 90 days 5. Video Viewers — 50%+ watched 6. Lead Form Openers (didn't submit) 7. Customer List — Upload email/phone list 8. Instagram Engagers — Last 90 days
  • Create Lookalike Audiences from customer list (1%, 3%, 5%)
  • Set up event tracking for Lead, Purchase, Contact

3Campaign Strategy & Structure

Google Ads Campaign Structure:

Campaign 1: SEARCH — High-Intent Keywords Ad Group 1: Brand Terms Keywords: "smilecare nyc", "smilecare dentist" Ad Group 2: General Service Keywords: "dentist new york", "dental clinic manhattan" Ad Group 3: Emergency Keywords: "emergency dentist nyc", "24 hour dentist" Ad Group 4: Specific Service Keywords: "teeth whitening nyc", "dental implants manhattan" Campaign 2: SEARCH — Competitor Terms Keywords: "[competitor name] reviews", "[competitor] alternative" Campaign 3: LOCAL SERVICES ADS (if eligible) Pay-per-lead, Google Guaranteed badge Campaign 4: PERFORMANCE MAX Assets: Headlines, descriptions, images, videos Goal: Lead generation Location: 15-25 mile radius Campaign 5: REMARKETING (Display/YouTube) Audience: Website visitors who didn't convert Creative: Reminder ads with social proof

Facebook Ads Campaign Structure:

Campaign 1: LEAD GENERATION (Top Priority) Ad Set 1: Lookalike 1% of customers Creative: Lead form with offer Ad Set 2: Interest targeting (local + service interest) Creative: Lead form with social proof Ad Set 3: Geographic targeting (zip codes within 15 mi) Creative: Video testimonial + lead form Campaign 2: RETARGETING Ad Set 1: Website visitors (30 days) Creative: "Still looking?" reminder ad Ad Set 2: Lead form openers (didn't submit) Creative: Urgency/scarcity ad Ad Set 3: Video viewers (50%+) Creative: Testimonial + CTA Campaign 3: BRAND AWARENESS (if budget allows) Ad Set 1: Broad local targeting Creative: Educational/value content Goal: Build retargeting audiences cheaply

PHASE 2: LAUNCH & OPTIMIZE (Week 2+)

5Landing Page Requirements

Landing Page Structure (for every ad campaign): 1. HEADLINE — Matches ad copy exactly "Get Professional Teeth Whitening FREE With Your First Visit" 2. TRUST BAR — Reviews, badges, certifications "4.9 Stars | 200+ Reviews | Google Guaranteed" 3. HERO IMAGE/VIDEO — Professional, relevant 4. BENEFITS — 3-4 key points with icons ✓ Same-day appointments ✓ Most insurance accepted ✓ No wait times ✓ Free parking 5. FORM — Short (Name, Phone, Email only) Above the fold on mobile! 6. TESTIMONIALS — 2-3 real patient reviews 7. FAQ — 3-4 common objections answered 8. FINAL CTA — Repeat the offer Conversion Optimization: - Load time < 3 seconds (test with PageSpeed Insights) - Mobile-first design - Phone number is clickable (tap-to-call) - Form has 3-4 fields MAX - Clear, contrasting CTA button color - Add urgency: "Only 5 spots left this week" - Trust badges near form (Google reviews, BBB, insurance logos)

6Weekly Optimization Checklist

Google Ads — Weekly:

  • Review Search Terms Report → Add irrelevant terms as negatives
  • Check keyword Quality Scores → Improve ads with score < 7
  • Pause keywords with high spend + zero conversions
  • Increase bids on high-converting keywords
  • Test new ad copy variations (always have 3+ per ad group)
  • Check geographic performance → Adjust location bids
  • Review device performance → Adjust mobile/desktop bids
  • Check time-of-day/day-of-week → Set ad schedule
  • Review competitor ads (Auction Insights report)

Facebook Ads — Weekly:

  • Check Cost Per Lead → Compare to target ($25-$50 for local)
  • Review ad frequency → If > 3, refresh creative or expand audience
  • Pause low-CTR ads (< 1%)
  • Test new images/videos (creative fatigue happens after 2-3 weeks)
  • A/B test: Headlines, images, audiences, placements
  • Check lead quality — are leads converting to customers?
  • Adjust daily budget based on performance
  • Check placement performance → Disable low-performing placements
