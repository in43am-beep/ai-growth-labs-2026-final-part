SEO BLOG POST — COMPLETE PROCESS

1Topic Research (30 minutes)

Step 1: Find Target Keyword Tools: Semrush Keyword Magic Tool, Ahrefs, Google Keyword Planner Process: 1. Enter service + location in keyword tool 2. Filter: Volume > 100, KD < 50 3. Look for long-tail opportunities 4. Check "People Also Ask" on Google 5. Check competitor blogs — what are THEY ranking for? Step 2: Validate the Topic - Google the keyword → Can you write something BETTER than page 1 results? - Check intent → Is the searcher looking for information, services, or comparison? - Match intent with your content type Step 3: Research PAA (People Also Ask) Google the keyword → Note every PAA question: These become your H2/H3 headings and FAQ section Example Keyword Research Output: Primary Keyword: "dental implants cost nyc" (Volume: 720, KD: 35) Secondary Keywords: - "how much do dental implants cost in new york" (320) - "affordable dental implants nyc" (210) - "dental implant financing nyc" (140) - "best dental implant dentist manhattan" (110) PAA Questions: - How much is a single dental implant in NYC? - Does insurance cover dental implants? - What is the cheapest way to get dental implants? - How long do dental implants last?

2Create Outline (20 minutes)

Blog Post Outline Template: Title: [Primary Keyword] — [Modifier: Complete Guide / 2026 / Cost / How To] Example: "Dental Implants Cost in NYC: Complete 2026 Price Guide" URL Slug: /dental-implants-cost-nyc/ Meta Description (155 chars): "How much do dental implants cost in NYC? Complete 2026 price guide with costs per tooth, financing options, and how to find the best implant dentist." Outline: H1: Dental Implants Cost in NYC: Complete 2026 Price Guide INTRO (150-200 words) - Hook with the question/problem - Quick answer upfront - What reader will learn - Include primary keyword in first 100 words H2: How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in NYC? H3: Single Tooth Implant Cost ($3,000-$5,000) H3: Multiple Teeth Implant Cost ($6,000-$15,000) H3: All-on-4 Full Arch ($15,000-$30,000) H3: Full Mouth Restoration ($30,000-$60,000) [Include comparison TABLE] H2: What's Included in the Cost? - Consultation and planning - Implant post - Abutment - Crown - Follow-up visits H2: Factors That Affect Pricing [Bullet list: location, dentist experience, materials, complexity] H2: Does Insurance Cover Dental Implants? [Direct answer + details] H2: Financing Options - CareCredit - In-house payment plans - FSA/HSA H2: How to Choose the Best Implant Dentist in NYC [Numbered list of criteria] H2: FAQ (5-8 questions from PAA) [FAQPage Schema markup] CONCLUSION + CTA - Summary - Book free consultation CTA - Phone number

3Write the Article (2-3 hours)

Writing Rules: 1. INTRO: Start with the answer, not fluff BAD: "When it comes to dental health, there are many important factors..." GOOD: "Dental implants in NYC cost between $3,000 and $6,000 per tooth in 2026." 2. SHORT PARAGRAPHS: Max 2-3 sentences per paragraph Mobile readers scroll quickly — walls of text kill engagement 3. USE SUBHEADINGS: Every 200-300 words, add an H2 or H3 Makes content scannable and SEO-friendly 4. TABLES & LISTS: Convert complex info into visual formats Cost comparisons, feature lists, pros/cons 5. STATISTICS: Include numbers with sources "According to the American Dental Association (2025), 78% of..." 6. INTERNAL LINKS: 3-5 per article Link to: service pages, related blog posts, booking page Use descriptive anchor text, NOT "click here" 7. EXTERNAL LINKS: 2-3 per article Link to: authoritative sources (ADA, Mayo Clinic, etc.) Opens in new tab 8. IMAGES: 3-5 per article Custom graphics, stock photos, or diagrams Every image needs descriptive alt text with keyword 9. E-E-A-T SIGNALS: - Author bio with credentials - Date published / last updated - Expert quotes - Cited sources - Real experience mentioned

4Optimize & Publish (30 minutes)

Pre-Publish SEO Checklist:

  • Primary keyword in Title tag (near beginning)
  • Primary keyword in H1
  • Primary keyword in first 100 words
  • Primary keyword in 2-3 H2 subheadings
  • Primary keyword in URL slug
  • Primary keyword in meta description
  • Primary keyword in image alt text
  • Keyword density: 1-2% (natural, not stuffed)
  • 3-5 internal links with descriptive anchor text
  • 2-3 external links to authoritative sources
  • 3-5 images with alt text
  • FAQPage Schema added for FAQ section
  • Article Schema added
  • Mobile-friendly formatting (short paragraphs)
  • Proofread for grammar (use Grammarly)
  • Word count: 2,000+ words

Post-Publish Distribution:

  • Submit URL to Google Search Console (Request Indexing)
  • Share on Facebook with engaging caption
  • Share on Instagram (carousel summary)
  • Share on LinkedIn
  • Create 3-5 social posts from the article
  • Send to email list
  • Share in relevant Facebook Groups
  • Update 2-3 older posts to link to this new article

Subject Line Formula: [Number/Question] + [Benefit] + [Curiosity] Examples: - "5 foods secretly destroying your teeth" - "Are you making this brushing mistake?" - "This month only: Free whitening for patients" Email Structure: 1. Hook (1-2 sentences) — Why they should read 2. Value (3-4 points) — Tips, news, education 3. CTA — Book, visit, read blog post 4. Social links — Follow us Example: Subject: "3 things your dentist wants you to stop doing" Hi [Name], Did you know that brushing right after eating acidic foods actually damages your enamel? Here are 3 common habits that might be hurting your teeth: 1. Brushing immediately after coffee or orange juice → Wait 30 minutes to let enamel re-harden 2. Using a hard-bristled toothbrush → Soft bristles clean just as well without enamel damage 3. Skipping your nighttime brush → Bacteria multiply 10x overnight without brushing Want to make sure your oral health is on track? [BOOK YOUR FREE CHECKUP →] See you at SmileCare NYC! Dr. Smith & Team

VIDEO SCRIPT TEMPLATE

Short-Form Video Script (30-60 seconds): HOOK (0-3 sec): "Stop doing THIS to your teeth!" / "POV: You're a dentist and..." PROBLEM (3-10 sec): State the common mistake or question SOLUTION (10-40 sec): Give the answer / tip / advice CTA (40-60 sec): "Follow for more tips!" / "Book link in bio" Example Script: [HOOK - On camera, pointing] "You're brushing your teeth WRONG. Here's why." [PROBLEM - Show brushing] "Most people brush in big circles or back-and-forth. This actually misses 40% of your tooth surface and can damage your gums." [SOLUTION - Demonstrate correct technique] "Instead, angle your brush at 45 degrees against the gumline. Use gentle circular motions. Each tooth gets 2-3 seconds. And ALWAYS start in the back." [CTA - Back on camera] "Follow @SmileCareNYC for more dental tips. Link in bio for a free checkup!" Tips: - Always add captions (85% watch without sound) - Use trending audio when possible - Film vertical (1080x1920) - Good lighting (natural or ring light) - Keep it under 60 seconds for best engagement
