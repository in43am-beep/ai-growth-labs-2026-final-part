Client Kickoff Call — Agenda, Script & Deliverables

Kickoff Call Template

Duration: 45-60 minutes | Schedule within 3 days of contract signing

PRE-CALL PREPARATION

Have These Ready Before the Call:

  • Completed audit report (PDF or screen-share ready)
  • 90-day roadmap document
  • Project management board set up (Trello/Monday/ClickUp)
  • Onboarding form reviewed — flag any missing info
  • All account access verified and working
  • Welcome email drafted
  • First month content calendar drafted
  • Competitor analysis summary ready

CALL AGENDA

0:00 - 5:00 — Welcome & Expectations

YOU: "Welcome aboard, [Name]! We're really excited to start working with you. The purpose of today's call is to:

1. Walk you through our audit findings
2. Present your 90-day roadmap
3. Set up communication and reporting expectations
4. Answer any questions you have

By the end of this call, you'll know exactly what we're doing, when, and what results to expect. Sound good?"

5:00 - 15:00 — Audit Presentation

YOU: "Let me share my screen and walk you through what we found..."

[Screen-share the audit report. Cover these points:]

1. Current Google Ranking: "When someone searches [service + city], here's where you're showing up... and here's where your competitors are."

2. Google Business Profile: "Your GBP is [X]% complete. Here's what's missing: [specifics]. Your competitors have [X] reviews at [X] stars."

3. Website Analysis: "Your site speed is [X]/100. Mobile experience is [X]. We found [X] technical issues to fix."

4. Competitor Gap: "Your biggest competitors are [A], [B], and [C]. Here's where they're ahead and where the opportunities are."

YOU: "The good news is — all of these are fixable, and we're going to tackle them systematically over the next 90 days."

15:00 - 30:00 — 90-Day Roadmap

1

Month 1: Foundation

Technical SEO, GBP setup, citations, on-page optimization

2

Month 2: Growth

Content publishing, link building, review generation

3

Month 3: Accelerate

Advanced SEO, rankings growth, ROI measurement

YOU: "Here's your 90-day roadmap. Let me walk you through what happens each month..."

MONTH 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1-4):
"Week 1: We complete the full technical audit and fix critical issues — site speed, mobile, broken links.
Week 2: We optimize all your page titles, meta descriptions, and add Schema markup.
Week 3: We begin building citations — getting your business listed on 30+ directories with perfect NAP consistency.
Week 4: We fully optimize your Google Business Profile and set up the review generation system."

MONTH 2 — Growth (Weeks 5-8):
"Week 5-6: We publish your first 4 SEO blog posts targeting your key services.
Week 7-8: We begin link building outreach and ramp up review generation. You'll receive your first monthly report."

MONTH 3 — Acceleration (Weeks 9-12):
"We analyze what's working, double down on winning strategies, expand to new keywords, and you'll start seeing measurable ranking improvements. We do a full 90-day review call to plan the next quarter."

30:00 - 40:00 — Communication & Reporting

YOU: "Let me explain how we'll communicate and keep you updated..."

Weekly Updates: "Every [day], you'll get a brief email with what we worked on and key wins."

Bi-Weekly Calls: "We'll have a 15-minute check-in every other [day] to stay aligned."

Monthly Reports: "On the first of each month, you'll get a detailed report covering rankings, traffic, reviews, and everything we did."

Monthly Strategy Call: "Once a month, we'll have a 30-minute deep dive to review results and adjust strategy."

Emergency Contact: "If anything urgent comes up — negative review, website issue — email or text me directly and we'll handle it within [X] hours."

YOU: "For project management, I've set you up on [Trello/Monday/ClickUp]. You'll be able to see exactly what we're working on at any time. I'll send you the invite right after this call."

40:00 - 50:00 — Set Expectations

YOU: "I want to be upfront about what to expect..."

Timeline: "SEO is not overnight. You'll start seeing improvements in weeks 4-6, but significant ranking changes typically happen in months 3-6. The results compound over time — month 6 will be much better than month 3."

What we need from you:
"1. Content approvals within 48 hours when we send drafts
2. Availability for our bi-weekly calls
3. Help with review generation — your front desk team asking happy customers
4. Any business changes (new services, hours, photos) communicated promptly"

What you can expect from us:
"1. Complete transparency — you'll always know what we're doing
2. Consistent communication — we never go silent
3. Measurable progress — data-driven, not guesswork
4. Quick response — within 4 business hours always"

50:00 - 60:00 — Q&A & Next Steps

YOU: "That covers everything for today. Do you have any questions at all?"

[Answer all questions thoroughly]

YOU: "Great! Here's what happens from here:

1. I'll send you the project management board invite today
2. We start technical fixes this week
3. You'll get your first weekly update on [day]
4. Our next check-in call is [date/time]

We're excited to get started. Let's make this the year your business dominates Google!"

POST-KICKOFF ACTIONS

  • Send kickoff call summary email within 2 hours
  • Send project management board invite
  • Send 90-day roadmap PDF
  • Add all tasks to project board (Month 1)
  • Begin technical audit execution
  • Schedule first bi-weekly check-in
  • Set up rank tracking for target keywords
  • Set up Google Analytics & Search Console monitoring
