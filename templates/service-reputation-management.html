3Create Google Review Link

Method 1 — From Google Maps: 1. Search client's business on Google Maps 2. Click "Write a review" 3. Copy the URL from the browser 4. Shorten with bit.ly Method 2 — Place ID Method: 1. Go to: developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id 2. Find Place ID for the business 3. Create link: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID 4. Shorten with bit.ly Method 3 — Google Business Profile: 1. Login to business.google.com 2. Go to Home → "Get more reviews" 3. Copy the short review link

PHASE 3: NEGATIVE REVIEW MANAGEMENT

6The A.R.E.S. Response Framework

A — Acknowledge: "We're sorry to hear about your experience..." R — Responsibility: "We take full responsibility for..." E — Empathy: "We understand how frustrating that must have been..." S — Solution: "Please contact us at [phone/email] so we can make this right"

Full Response Example:

"We're so sorry to hear about your experience, [Name]. This absolutely doesn't reflect the level of care and service we strive to provide at SmileCare NYC. We understand how frustrating [specific issue they mentioned] must have been, and we take your feedback very seriously. We'd love the opportunity to make this right. Please contact our office manager, [Name], directly at (212) 555-0123 or email info@smilecarenyc.com so we can address your concerns personally. Thank you for bringing this to our attention — it helps us improve."

7Review Removal Process

When Can You Flag a Review for Removal?

  • Spam or fake content (never visited the business)
  • Conflict of interest (competitor, ex-employee)
  • Offensive / hate speech / profanity
  • Off-topic (not about the business experience)
  • Personal information exposed
Removal Process: 1. Go to Google Maps → Find the review 2. Click the 3 dots next to the review → "Flag as inappropriate" 3. Select the reason 4. Submit 5. Wait 5-14 days for Google's review 6. If denied → Appeal once through Google Business support 7. If still denied → Focus on burying it with positive reviews
⚠️ Never: Buy fake reviews, offer incentives for reviews, ask only happy customers (review gating), write reviews yourself, use review farms/fiverr, threaten reviewers
