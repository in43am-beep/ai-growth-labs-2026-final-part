How to optimize businesses to be cited, recommended, and featured by ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and AI Overviews.

UNDERSTANDING AI SEO

What AI Systems Look For When Recommending Businesses:

  1. Entity Recognition — Does the business exist as a "known entity" online?
  2. Authoritative Mentions — Is the business mentioned on trusted websites?
  3. Structured Data — Can AI easily parse business information?
  4. Review Volume & Quality — High ratings signal trustworthiness
  5. Content Authority — Does the website provide expert, factual information?
  6. Recency — Is information current and updated?
  7. Geographic Relevance — Is the business clearly tied to a location?

PHASE 2: ENTITY BUILDING (Weeks 2-4)

3Knowledge Graph & Entity Signals

A. Wikidata Entry:

Steps: 1. Go to wikidata.org → Create account 2. Click "Create a new Item" 3. Fill in: - Label: SmileCare NYC - Description: Dental practice in Manhattan, New York - Also known as: SmileCare, SmileCare Dental 4. Add properties: - Instance of: Dental practice (Q4116214) - Country: United States (Q30) - Located in: Manhattan (Q11299) - Official website: https://smilecarenyc.com - Inception: 2010 - Phone: +1-212-555-0123 Note: Wikidata entries must be notable. For local businesses, having press coverage or significant online presence helps.

B. Google Knowledge Panel:

How to get/claim a Knowledge Panel: 1. Search your business name on Google 2. If panel exists → Click "Claim this knowledge panel" 3. If doesn't exist, build signals: - Perfect NAP consistency across 50+ citations - Comprehensive Schema markup on website - Wikipedia/Wikidata presence - Brand mentions in news/press - All social profiles linked consistently 4. Use Google's Entity Home: https://knowledgepanel.google.com

C. Brand Mention Campaign:

AI learns from mentions across the web. Build mentions through: 1. Press Releases: (1-2 per month) - New service launches - Awards/recognition - Community involvement - Hire announcements Tools: PRWeb ($99-$389/release), Newswire, PRNewswire 2. Industry Publications: - Guest articles on industry blogs - Expert quotes in articles (use HARO/Connectively) - Interview podcasts - Contribute to industry roundups 3. Local News Coverage: - Pitch local interest stories - Sponsor local events - Community involvement press - Expert commentary on local issues 4. Forum & Discussion Presence: - Reddit (relevant subreddits) - Quora (answer industry questions) - Industry forums - Facebook Groups Note: Add genuine value, don't spam

PHASE 3: AI-OPTIMIZED CONTENT (Ongoing)

4Content That AI Loves to Cite

Content Format Rules for AI Citation: 1. DIRECT ANSWERS — Start with the answer, then elaborate BAD: "There are many factors to consider when thinking about..." GOOD: "Dental implants in NYC cost $3,000-$6,000 per implant in 2026." 2. STRUCTURED DATA — Use lists, tables, numbered steps AI prefers: Bullet points, comparison tables, step-by-step guides 3. STATISTICS & DATA — Include specific numbers BAD: "Most people prefer professional whitening" GOOD: "78% of patients prefer in-office whitening over at-home kits (ADA 2025)" 4. FAQ FORMAT — Question + concise answer AI frequently pulls from FAQ sections 5. ORIGINAL RESEARCH — AI loves citing first-party data Example: "Our survey of 500 NYC dental patients found..." 6. EXPERT ATTRIBUTION — Quote real professionals "According to Dr. Smith, DDS, with 15 years of experience..."
