CLIENT COMMUNICATION BEST PRACTICES

Communication Schedule: WEEKLY: Brief email update (3-5 bullet points) "Hi [Name], here's your weekly update: • Published blog post on dental implants (Link) • 3 new Google reviews received (now at 188) • Moved from #8 to #5 for 'dentist nyc' • Built 10 new citations this week • Next week: launching Google Ads campaign" BI-WEEKLY: 15-minute check-in call - Quick wins to celebrate - Any client concerns - Alignment on priorities MONTHLY: 30-minute strategy call + full report - Complete performance review - Next month strategy - Answer all questions QUARTERLY: 60-minute deep dive - 90-day look-back - ROI analysis - Strategy adjustments - Contract renewal / upsell discussion RESPONSE TIME STANDARDS: - Email: Within 4 hours (business hours) - Phone/urgent: Within 1 hour - Slack/chat: Within 2 hours - Reports: Delivered 2 days before scheduled call - Negative review alerts: Within 2 hours

Client Retention Tips

  • Over-communicate early — First 30 days, update more than necessary
  • Set realistic expectations — Under-promise, over-deliver
  • Celebrate wins — Send excited messages when rankings improve
  • Send handwritten notes — Birthday cards, anniversary cards, holiday gifts
  • Be proactive — Don't wait for clients to ask, share ideas first
  • Ask for feedback — Quarterly satisfaction surveys
  • Show ROI — Always tie your work to revenue/leads
  • Answer fast — Speed of response = perceived value
💡 It costs 5x more to acquire a new client than to retain an existing one. Focus on retention.

Phase 1: Solo (Months 1-6)

  • Do all work yourself — learn every process deeply
  • Use templates and SOPs to systematize everything
  • Charge $997-$2,997/client/month
  • Focus on getting 3-5 amazing results (case studies)
  • Ask every happy client for referrals
  • Build your personal brand (LinkedIn posts, blog)

Phase 2: Small Team (Months 6-18)

  • Hire Virtual Assistant ($500-$1,000/mo) — citations, data entry, reporting
  • Hire Content Writer ($500-$2,000/mo) — blog posts, social content
  • You focus on strategy, client calls, and sales
  • Create detailed SOPs for every task (so team can execute)
  • Start building systems: templates, checklists, workflows

Phase 3: Full Agency (Year 2-3)

  • Hire Account Manager — client communication and reporting
  • Hire SEO Specialist — technical SEO, on-page, link building
  • Hire Social Media Manager — content creation, engagement
  • Hire Ad Specialist — Google Ads, Facebook Ads
  • You focus on sales, strategy, and business development

Phase 4: Growth (Year 3+)

  • Department heads manage teams
  • Sales team bringing in new clients
  • White-label partnerships with other agencies
  • Multiple service tiers and upsell paths
  • You focus on vision, partnerships, and growth

PRICING & PACKAGING STRATEGY

The 3-Tier Pricing Psychology: TIER 1 — STARTER (Foot in the door) Price: $997-$1,497/month Purpose: Get clients started, prove your value Include: Basic SEO + GBP + basic reporting Target: Small businesses with limited budget TIER 2 — GROWTH (Sweet spot — most popular) Price: $2,497-$3,997/month Purpose: Full-service package for serious businesses Include: Everything in Starter + Content + Reviews + Link Building Target: Established businesses ready to grow Mark this as "MOST POPULAR" — anchoring effect TIER 3 — DOMINATION (Premium upsell) Price: $4,997-$6,997/month Purpose: Everything + Ads + Social + AI SEO Include: Full-service digital marketing Target: Multi-location, high-revenue businesses WHY 3 TIERS WORK: - Tier 1: Makes you accessible (low barrier) - Tier 2: Most people choose middle (anchoring) - Tier 3: Makes Tier 2 seem affordable (contrast) - 60-70% will choose Tier 2 (highest margin) UPSELL STRATEGY: Month 1-3: Client on Tier 1 → Show results Month 4: Present growth data → Recommend Tier 2 Month 6+: Show expanded opportunities → Suggest Tier 3 or add-ons ADD-ON SERVICES (additional revenue): - Extra blog posts: $150-$300 each - Landing page design: $500-$1,000 each - Video creation: $200-$500 each - Extra cities/locations: $300-$500/month each - Press release: $200-$400 each - Website redesign: $2,000-$5,000 (one-time)

HOW TO GET YOUR FIRST 10 CLIENTS

Strategy 1: FREE AUDITS (Best for starting) - Find local businesses with poor Google presence - Create a free audit showing their problems - Email/call them with the audit attached - Offer free 15-minute call to discuss - Convert to paid client - Target: Send 10 audits/week → Close 1-2 clients/month Strategy 2: LOCAL NETWORKING - Join Chamber of Commerce - Attend BNI or local business networking groups - Attend industry events - Build relationships with complementary businesses (web designers, business coaches, accountants) - Ask for referrals Strategy 3: LINKEDIN OUTREACH - Connect with local business owners - Share valuable content (case studies, tips) - Comment on their posts - Send personalized DMs (NOT spam) - Offer free audit or strategy session Strategy 4: YOUR OWN SEO - Rank your own website for "[service] + [your city]" - Example: "SEO agency Dallas" or "reputation management NYC" - Practice what you preach — proves credibility - Takes 3-6 months but generates leads forever Strategy 5: REFERRAL PROGRAM - Offer existing clients $500-$1,000 for referrals - Or offer 1 month free for successful referral - Most agencies get 40-60% of new clients from referrals Strategy 6: SOCIAL PROOF MARKETING - Post case study results on LinkedIn, Instagram - Create "before/after" content showing client results - Record video testimonials from happy clients - Share screenshots of ranking improvements Target: 2-3 new clients per month until you hit capacity

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

  1. Underpricing — Don't charge $300/month. You can't deliver quality at that price. $997 minimum.
  2. Over-promising — Never say "I'll get you to #1 in 30 days." Set realistic expectations.
  3. Taking bad clients — Learn to say no. Difficult clients drain energy and aren't worth it.
  4. Not tracking results — If you can't show ROI, you'll lose clients. Track everything.
  5. Doing everything manually — Automate repetitive tasks ASAP. Your time = money.
  6. Ignoring your own marketing — If your own website doesn't rank, why would clients trust you?
  7. Not getting testimonials early — Ask happy clients in month 2-3 for testimonials and case studies.
  8. No SOPs — Document every process. When you hire, SOPs let others replicate your quality.
  9. Scope creep — Define deliverables clearly. Extra work = extra charges.
  10. Slow communication — Respond within 4 hours. Silence kills client relationships.

Remember

"Quality of service beats quantity of clients every time. 5 happy clients who refer you will grow your agency faster than 20 unhappy ones."
